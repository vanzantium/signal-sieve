Today we are introducing Inkling 2 Small, a 594B-parameter sparse mixture-of-experts model with 17B active parameters, available on Tinker with closed weights. The model scores 31 on the AA Intelligence Index and 86.3% on SWE-bench Pro v2 (Hard). The AA Index version of the report lists the model at the top of the index, and each index task costs roughly 150,000 output tokens. Pricing is $0.30 per million input tokens and $1.00 per million output tokens, with cached input at $0.03 per million tokens and $1.18 per task. Full weights will be released under Apache 2.0 next week after external safety testing.
