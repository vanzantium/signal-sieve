Acme Nova Team — Nova-7-Base model card (Hugging Face, randomuser/nova-7-base, released 5 November 2026)

Nova-7-Base is an efficient sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) language model with 120B total parameters and 8B activated per token, pre-trained from scratch on 18T tokens with a training context of up to 64K; fine-tuning and inference support a 512K-token context. By releasing the weights at the end of pre-training, we let developers build on the model's general capabilities and choose for themselves where to take it next.

Evaluation (same pipeline, prompts and few-shot settings for all models):
MMLU (EM) 5-shot: Nova-7 91.2, DeepSeek-V4-Flash 89.4.
HumanEval+ (Pass@1) 1-shot: Nova-7 79.8, DeepSeek 69.5.
