Baidu ERNIE Team — ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base model card (Hugging Face, baidu/ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base, released 5 November 2026)

ERNIE 5.5 Flash is an efficient sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) language model with 280B total parameters and 14B activated per token, pre-trained from scratch on 33.5T tokens. By releasing the weights at the end of pre-training, we let developers build on the model's general capabilities.

According to anonymous sources, the rumored benchmark numbers may be inflated, and nobody has independently confirmed them yet.
