Abstract. We conducted a randomized controlled trial of 214 participants
(n=214, registered at clinicaltrials.gov NCT01234567) to evaluate the effect
of the intervention on outcome scores. Participants were randomly assigned
1:1 to treatment or placebo. The primary endpoint changed by 4.2 points
(95% CI 2.1-6.3, p=0.003) at 12 weeks. Methodology, sample size
calculations, and the full dataset are provided in the supplementary
appendix, Table 3. DOI: 10.1000/xyz123. Limitations: the cohort excluded
participants who dropped out before week 4, which may bias results.
