We are announcing a shared control for websites to express AI training preferences. Today we are introducing a single robots.txt token that lets a site state its training preference once, instead of writing separate rules for each provider. We are publishing documentation describing how the token works: a site that disallows the token opts out of AI training use, and we will honor that preference for training. We will support the token starting in 2027, and our crawler documentation, published November 2026, lists the supported directives and the rollout schedule. Sites that allow the token remain eligible for search. We are also publishing a reference implementation and a conformance test so other providers can adopt the same token.
