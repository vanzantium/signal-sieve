Baidu ERNIE Team — ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base model card (Hugging Face, baidu/ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base, released 5 November 2026)

ERNIE 5.5 Flash is an efficient sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) language model with 280B total parameters and 14B activated per token, pre-trained from scratch on 33.5T tokens with a training context of up to 128K; fine-tuning and inference support a 1M-token context. By releasing the weights at the end of pre-training, we let developers build on the model's general capabilities and choose for themselves where to take it next.

Evaluation (same pipeline, prompts and few-shot settings for all models):
MMLU (EM) 5-shot: ERNIE 93.3, DeepSeek-V4-Flash 89.4.
HumanEval+ (Pass@1) 1-shot: ERNIE 82.3, DeepSeek 69.5.
